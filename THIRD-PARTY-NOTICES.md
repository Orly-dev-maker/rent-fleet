# Third-party notices

## Car images

**This package** contains a single generated grey placeholder image, `public/images/cars/placeholder-01.jpg`,
shown for vehicles without photos. It contains no third-party content. Add your own car photos from
`/admin/vehicles` (see `public/images/cars/README.txt`).

**The online demo** displays photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). These photographs are **not** included in this
package.

## 3D models

The optional 3D cars of the showroom (`/showroom/[id]`) are generated by code in this project
(`components/CarModel.tsx`, `lib/carSpecs.ts`). No third-party 3D asset is bundled.
